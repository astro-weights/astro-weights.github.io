Privacy

Covers astro-weights.dev: the home page, /docs/ and /blog/. Last changed 2026-11-19.

Who runs this site

The astro-weights project. Contact: privacy@astro-weights.dev.

What we collect

Only if you click Accept, we load Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager. It records pages viewed, scrolling, clicks on outside links, file downloads, form and video interactions, site search terms, approximate location, and device and browser. Your IP address is sent to Google with each request. If you click Reject or make no choice, none of this loads.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets two cookies, _ga and _ga_<id>, each kept for 2 years. We store your choice in your browser's local storage so we don't ask on every page. If you withdraw, Google Analytics stops loading; cookies it already set stay in your browser until they expire or you clear them.

Why

To count visits and see which pages help people, so we can improve the site. The legal basis is your consent.

Who receives it

Google LLC. Data may be processed in the United States. How Google uses data from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

How long

Google Analytics keeps event data, such as pages viewed and clicks, for 2 months. Data tied to your browser's analytics ID is kept for up to 14 months, and that period starts again each time you come back.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address for security. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Product Hunt badge

The footer of the home page and the /adapters/ pages shows a Product Hunt badge with a live upvote count. The badge image loads from Product Hunt (api.producthunt.com) when you scroll near the footer, whether or not you accept analytics, so Product Hunt receives your IP address and browser details. See the Product Hunt privacy and cookies policy.

What we don't do

No ads, no ad personalisation, and we don't sell data.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to limit or object to its use, or to get a copy. You can withdraw consent at any time with Cookie settings in the footer of any page; this does not affect use before you withdrew. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
