What we collect

Only if you click Accept, we load Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager. It records pages viewed, scrolling, clicks on outside links, file downloads, form and video interactions, site search terms, approximate location, and device and browser. Your IP address is sent to Google with each request. If you click Reject or make no choice, none of this loads.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets two cookies, _ga and _ga_<id>, each kept for 2 years. We store your choice in your browser's local storage so we don't ask on every page. If you withdraw, Google Analytics stops loading; cookies it already set stay in your browser until they expire or you clear them.

Why

To count visits and see which pages help people, so we can improve the site. The legal basis is your consent.

Who receives it

Google LLC. Data may be processed in the United States. How Google uses data from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
